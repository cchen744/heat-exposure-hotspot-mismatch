# Twelve-city deep relationship notes

## Main paper direction
The 12-city comparison suggests that LST and Heat Index do not identify identical hotspot geographies. The mismatch is structured by urban-climate context, with water-influenced/coastal cities showing stronger apparent-heat hotspot shifts, while desert and transitional cities show more coupled or mixed LST-HI patterns.

## Strongest current paper storyline
A strong paper framing is: surface temperature and apparent heat reveal different urban heat-risk mechanisms. Across 12 cities, apparent-heat hotspots are especially prominent in coastal/water-influenced cities, while some inland/desert cities show stronger LST-HI coupling. This suggests that relying only on LST may miss humid or water-influenced heat-risk geographies.
