# RQ1 manuscript map

## Core message
The spatial overlap between Land Surface Temperature (LST) hotspots and Heat Index (HI) hotspots is incomplete in both Houston and Phoenix. This suggests that LST alone does not fully capture humidity-sensitive heat exposure.
